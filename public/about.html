A tiny game for your tea break.

Two café games. No account needed.

Snack Sneak: eat while the boss looks away. Tray Chaos: catch tips and dodge pigeons.

Small enough for a short break

The game runs in your browser without an account. It works with touch, mouse or keyboard, and the daily challenge gives everyone the same rush to tackle. Your best score stays on your device.

Original work

The game logic, café scene and results are made for Snack Sneak. The food sprites were created for this game with AI image generation. The display and body fonts are Changa One and DM Sans, distributed under their open font licenses. The café is fictional and is not affiliated with a restaurant or drink brand.

給茶點時間的一點小混亂。

Snack Sneak 是一款原創網頁小遊戲：趁老闆睇手機偷食蛋撻、菠蘿油、西多士、沙嗲牛肉公仔麵同火腿蛋三文治。老闆回頭前放手，睇吓 30 秒食到幾多。另有 Tray Chaos，接小費、避鴿子。

不用帳號，手機、滑鼠和鍵盤都可以玩。每日挑戰讓大家遇到同一場午市，個人紀錄只存在你的裝置。遊戲中的茶餐廳是虛構的，與任何餐廳或飲品品牌無關。
