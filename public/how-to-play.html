Snack SneakTray Chaos

Eat fast. Act innocent.

Hold the button, food or Space to eat. Let go before the boss looks.

Watch the colour

Green: eat. Yellow: let go. Red: hands off.

Score

Each bite: 10 points. Finish a dish: +120. Eat, then release just before a look: +60.

Caught: −80 points and lose the dish. Release before eating again. Three catches end the round; otherwise, you get 30 seconds.

Pause and replay

Press P, Escape or Pause. Switching tabs also pauses. Daily resets at Hong Kong midnight. Practice replays the same run.

Share

After a round, choose Threads, X or WhatsApp, or save an image. For Instagram Stories, save the vertical image and add the copied challenge link with a Link sticker.

Scores stay on your device. Shared scores are unverified challenges.

Play

玩法

按住按鈕、食物或空白鍵偷食。綠色可以食，黃色要放手，紅色唔好郁。

每口 10 分，食完一份加 120 分。食過再壓秒放手，加 60 分。被抓扣 80 分並沒收食物，要先放手先可再食。三次被抓就收工，一局最多 30 秒。

P、Escape 或切換分頁可暫停。每日挑戰於香港午夜更新。成績只存喺本機，收工後可以分享。
