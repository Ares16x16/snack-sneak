Snack SneakTray Chaos

A steady hand goes a long way.

Milk tea, matcha, an egg tart, and a gang of deeply unprofessional pigeons. You have 30 seconds to catch tips, protect lunch and cause a little chaos.

Move the tray

On your phone, drag across the scene or hold the Left and Right buttons. On a keyboard, hold ← / → or A / D. The tray moves sideways and leans as you accelerate. Line up underneath gold tips and falling tarts.

Do not feed the pigeons

A pigeon that reaches your tray steals a snack and breaks your catch streak. Move out of its way, or press AIYA! / Space to shoo every pigeon currently on screen. Your shout recharges in five seconds, so save it for a bird that deserves it.

Catch, combo, rescue

A tip earns 50 points and a tart earns 90. Consecutive catches raise the multiplier every three catches, up to ×4. Missing a treat or getting robbed breaks the streak. Catching a tart restores a lost snack, or tops up your drinks when all three are still on board.

Your shift receipt

A dodge earns 20 points and a shooed pigeon earns 25. At the end, add 5 points for every second survived, 150 for each snack saved and up to 100 for preservation. Losing all three snacks ends your shift early. Your receipt records what actually survived, your best catch streak and how many pigeons you evicted.

Daily or practice

The daily challenge uses the same sequence of tips, tarts and pigeons for everyone and changes at midnight in Hong Kong (UTC+8). A challenge link replays that day's tray, even on another day. Practice picks a different sequence when you choose the mode; replaying your receipt repeats the same tray so you can improve.

Take a tea break

Use Pause, press P or Escape, or switch away from the tab. The game pauses without spending your remaining time. Your personal best is stored only in this browser. Clearing browser data or changing devices starts a new local record.

Send your shift receipt

At the end, save your result image or use Share result. If your browser cannot share an image directly, copy the challenge link and post it with the saved image. Shared scores are friendly, unverified targets rather than a public leaderboard.

奶茶要保，鴿子要趕。

30 秒內左右移動托盤，接住金色小費和蛋撻，避開來搶午餐的鴿子。手機可在場景拖曳或按住左右按鈕，電腦可用 ← → / A D。

按 AIYA! 或空白鍵，可趕走畫面上的鴿子，每 5 秒可用一次。鴿子撞到托盤會搶走茶點並打斷連接，別讓牠們白吃白喝。

小費 50 分，蛋撻 90 分。連續每接住三次，分數倍率就提高，最高 4 倍。蛋撻可救回一款失去的茶點；全員都在時則補滿一點飲品。閃開鴿子得 20 分，大叫趕走每隻鴿子得 25 分。

收工另計每秒 5 分、每款保住的茶點 150 分，以及最多 100 分的保存獎勵。每日挑戰於香港時間午夜更新；同一天的連結會遇上同一批鴿子。按 P、Escape 或切換分頁可暫停。
