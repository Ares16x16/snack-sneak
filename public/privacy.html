Your snack. Your data.

Updated 4 November 2026

You can play Snack Sneak without creating an account. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location or payment details.

What stays in your browser

Local storage remembers your language, sound preference and personal best scores. These are saved on your device, not in a game leaderboard or account. You can remove them by clearing this site's browser data. If storage is unavailable, you can still play.

What a challenge link contains

Challenge links contain the game, date or practice seed, score and language. Anyone with the link can see them. Images are made on your device. Threads, X and WhatsApp buttons open that service with the score and link. You finish the post or message there. Instagram Stories use a saved image or your device's share menu.

Hosting

When you visit a website, its hosting provider receives technical request information such as an IP address and browser details to deliver and protect the site. GitHub Pages is subject to GitHub's privacy statement; a Cloudflare deployment is subject to Cloudflare's privacy policy. No game account or server-side score collection is part of this version.

Optional analytics

If PostHog analytics is enabled, pressing Play starts usage measurement. You can switch Analytics off in the footer before playing. It shares page visits, a coarse traffic source, game starts, scores, outcomes, active playtime, pauses, replays and share-button clicks. Paused and background time is excluded. Share clicks do not prove that a post was published. Turning it off does not affect the game.

We send only defined events, without names, emails, recordings, form contents or full URL queries. PostHog receives technical connection information when events are delivered. Location enrichment is disabled for these events. A random browser identifier expires after 90 days; a tab session identifier and your analytics preference are stored on your device. The identifiers are not linked to a game account. See PostHog's privacy policy for its processing.

Use Analytics in the footer to change your choice when analytics is available. Turning it off stops future events and removes the analytics identifiers from this browser; it does not delete events already received by PostHog. Contact us for questions or deletion requests. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track disable collection. Local previews never send analytics events.

Advertising

Advertising is disabled. Before adding ads, we will update this notice and the relevant consent choices. Google's advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies; see Google's explanation of advertising technologies.

Questions

For questions, reply to the post or message where you found the game.

挑戰連結可能包含日期或練習種子、目標分數和語言。接收連結的人都能看見這些內容。成績圖片喺你嘅裝置製作。撳 Threads、X 或 WhatsApp 會開啟該服務並填入分數同連結，由你完成發佈。IG 限時動態可用儲存圖片或裝置分享功能。

廣告未啟用。如果網站已設定 PostHog，撳開局就會記錄瀏覽、遊玩時間、分數、結果、重玩同分享按鈕紀錄。可以先喺頁尾關閉數據分析，關咗一樣玩得。暫停同背景時間唔會計入遊玩時間。唔會收集姓名、電郵、表單內容、完整網址查詢或畫面錄影；PostHog 會收到傳送事件所需嘅連線資料，事件已停用位置補充。隨機瀏覽器識別碼 90 日後到期，分析設定同分頁識別碼存在你嘅瀏覽器。可喺頁尾「數據分析」隨時關閉並清除本機分析識別碼；已送出嘅事件唔會因此刪除，可聯絡我哋查詢。瀏覽器嘅 GPC／Do Not Track 會停用收集，本機預覽唔會傳送數據。
